# Security policy

Please do not report security issues in public issues or pull requests.

Report them privately through GitHub: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. The report reaches me directly and stays private until a fix is out.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the version or commit. I aim to acknowledge reports within 72 hours and to agree on a disclosure date with you. Credit is given in the advisory unless you prefer otherwise.
